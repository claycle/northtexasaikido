## Evening Class Time Change

The new traffic patterns from all our new neighbors trying to get home have more than doubled the time it takes to get to the Dojo on weeknights. What was once a 20 minute ride out to the countryside is now taking close to an hour. We’ve polled everyone at the Dojo, and we’re all in agreement to give a later start time a try in the hopes of a less frustrating commute to class. So, starting tonight, we shifting the class back a half-hour to start at 8pm.
